Goal
Build a scraper that collects pages from a practice site, extracts and cleans the useful fields, and saves structured records — while behaving like a bot the site's owner would allow.

Purpose
A huge share of "AI applications" are really data-gathering applications with a model attached — and nobody teaches the gathering. This is the workshop pipeline (fetch → parse → extract → clean → structure) built by your own hands, with the professionalism layer: robots.txt, rate limits, identification.
